🧠About

Notevora is your all-in-one academic companion built specifically for undergraduate students. Designed to be intuitive and clutter-free, Notevora empowers you to organize, and absorb knowledge — all in one place.

🎯Our Mission

To revolutionize the studying experience by making reliable, high-quality study materials (notes, books, MST papers, previous exams) instantly accessible, while offering powerful tools that help students to stay on top of their academics.
